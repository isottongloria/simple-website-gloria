Experience

I am currently a PhD student in the group of Elisa Ricci at the University of Trento. My research focuses on sign language video generation, human motion generation, and more broadly on video synthesis and machine learning for human-centered AI.

Core Competencies

  • Programming & Tools: Python, C++, R, Bash, MySQL, LATEX, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Numpy, Matplotlib, Linux.
  • Data Science & AI: Machine learning, deep learning, GANs, HMMs, V&L models, RAG, RBMs.
  • Scientific Computing: Numerical methods, Bayesian optimization, simulations of complex systems.
  • Visualization & Communication: Scientific visualization, LATEX writing, documentation tools.

Portfolio
