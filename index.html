Experience

As a Master’s student in Physics of Data at the University of Padua, I have developed a strong interdisciplinary foundation in computational physics, data science, and artificial intelligence. My expertise lies at the intersection of theoretical modeling and practical machine learning applications.

Core Competencies

  • Programming & Tools: Proficient in Python, C++, R, Bash, MySQL, and LATEX. Experienced with PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Numpy, Matplotlib, and Linux environments.
  • Data Science & AI: Hands-on experience in machine learning, deep learning, and data analysis. Worked with models such as GANs, Restricted Boltzmann Machines, V&L models, Hidden Markov Models, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
  • Scientific Computing: Solid foundation in numerical methods, Bayesian optimization, and complex systems modeling. Simulated systems such as the 2D Ising model and SIRV epidemiological models on scale-free networks.
  • Visualization & Communication: Skilled in scientific visualization (e.g., Ovito), academic writing with LATEX, and collaborative documentation (Obsidian, Office).

Portfolio

Here are some of the most significant academic and personal projects I've worked on during my studies:

  • GAN Music Generator – Developed a Generative Adversarial Network inspired by MIDINet to generate original symbolic-domain music compositions.
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation on Ulysses – Enhanced a LLaMA-based model with external knowledge from James Joyce’s *Ulysses* to improve contextual QA performance.
  • Unsupervised Learning by competing hidden units – Experimented with biologically plausible learning algorithms for image classification.
  • Bayesian Optimization – Applied Bayesian methods to tune parameters for image classification and simulation models.
  • COVID-19 Vaccine Hesitancy Simulation – Modeled vaccine hesitancy using a SIRV framework on a Barabási-Albert network to study spread patterns.
  • Neural Style Transfer – Used CNNs to blend the content of photos with the artistic style of paintings, based on Gatys’ method.
  • K-Means Parallel Clustering – Implemented scalable clustering using PySpark on Cloud Veneto infrastructure.
  • Hidden Markov Models for Protein Dynamics – Estimated hidden states in protein conformational time series using HMMs.
  • Restricted Boltzmann Machines – Trained RBMs with contrastive divergence for distribution learning on categorical data.
  • 2D Ising Model Simulation – Simulated critical phenomena using Glauber dynamics, Wolff algorithm, and parallel tempering.
